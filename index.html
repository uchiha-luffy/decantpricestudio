Pricing formulas used
1. Full-bottle selling price = buying price + target profit
2. Retail value per ml = full-bottle selling price ÷ full-bottle volume
3. Decant price = (retail value per ml × decant size) + empty bottle cost + packaging cost
4. Estimated decant profit = rounded selling price − perfume cost − empty bottle cost − packaging cost
Suggested prices are rounded up to the next whole currency unit.
Prices update automatically as you type.
Default formula
Suggested price = (full-bottle selling price ÷ bottle ml × decant ml) + empty decant bottle cost. This keeps the perfume's per-ml selling rate consistent and adds packaging.
